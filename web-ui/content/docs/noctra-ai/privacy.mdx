## What Stays on Your Device

- **No developer backend**: NOCTRA AI does not connect to any NOCTRA-operated server.
- **No analytics or ads**: Zero telemetry SDKs, crash uploaders, or ad trackers are bundled.
- **Biometric Lock Gate**: Optional device authentication (`local_auth`) protects app access on-device.

## What Is Transmitted Externally

When you send a chat message to an external AI provider (for example a cloud API endpoint), your prompt, any selected conversation history, and any staged attachment text/images are transmitted directly from your Android device to that provider's servers.

> Always review the privacy policy and data retention terms of the third-party AI provider you configure.
