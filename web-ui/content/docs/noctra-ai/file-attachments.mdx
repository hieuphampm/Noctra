## Supported Formats

NOCTRA AI processes attachments locally on your Android device before building the request payload:

- **Images**: `JPG`, `JPEG`, `PNG`, `WEBP`
- **Documents**: `PDF`, `DOCX`, `XLSX`, `PPTX`
- **Text & Data**: `TXT`, `MD`, `JSON`, `CSV`

## Local Extraction Pipeline

Text-bearing documents (`PDF`, Office XML archives, and plain-text formats) are parsed on-device so you can inspect staged content in the attachment preview modal before sending.

## Transmission Control

Attached files are only included in outbound requests when you stage them in the composer and send the message, subject to your per-conversation attachment transmission toggles.
